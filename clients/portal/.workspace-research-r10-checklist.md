## Anti-patterns to reject (Workspace)

1. Second 240px settings sidebar inside Settings.  
2. Advertising Roles / SSO in IA, pills, or ⌘K.  
3. Two live compliance UIs (`audit-log` tab body + `/audit-trail`).  
4. Mobile More “workspace” pointing at Artifacts / Alerts / Ledger.  
5. Team-only chrome (invites, SCIM, transfer) on personal orgs.  
6. Hand-rolled accent CTAs / non-DESIGN.md buttons on Workspace leaves.  
7. Dumping all Advanced knobs on first Settings paint.  
8. Overview as a second product home (Observe / Eval / Improve loops).  
9. Breaking `?tab=` deep links without redirects.  
10. Shipping leaf rewrites before IA / ghost / audit contracts (Wave 1).
