## Behavior

1. **First visit:** banner (full or short-page collapse pill) until Accept / Decline.
2. **After choice:** component renders **null** — no fixed “Cookie preferences” thumb-zone pill.
3. **Reopen:**
   - Settings → Account → **Cookie preferences** → Manage cookies
   - Marketing footer legal → **Cookie preferences**
   - Both dispatch `eden:open-cookie-preferences` (`openCookiePreferences()` in `analytics.ts`)
4. **Withdraw:** reopen → Decline → `applyAnalyticsConsent("declined")` + storage update → hide again.
