Privacy

Eden is built around the principle that your telemetry is yours. This page summarises what we collect, where it lives, who can see it, and how to take it back. For the legally-binding version, see our DPA.

Draft for review — clauses on this page have not received final counsel sign-off. Do not rely on this text for contractual purposes until the published DPA is countersigned.

1. What we collect

Eden processes two distinct categories of data: Account Data (your sign-up email, name, organisation membership, and authentication credentials) and Telemetry Data (the LLM calls, prompts, completions, and tool invocations that flow through the Eden gateway when you integrate the SDK or proxy).

By default, request and response bodies are not persisted to disk. Eden captures structured metadata — model, token counts, latency, status, custom spans — that is sufficient for observability and eval workloads. You opt in to full body capture per agent if you want replay or eval-on-trace features.

2. PII handling

Before persistence, every captured body passes through the PII redaction filter declared in your PII policy. The default policy masks emails, phone numbers, credit-card numbers, SSNs, and IP addresses. You can extend the policy with custom regex patterns or disable redaction per category.

PII redaction is irreversible — the original payload is dropped at the gateway edge and never reaches our storage tier. For compliance-sensitive workloads, contact us about dedicated tenancy (enterprise).

3. Data residency

Hosted customers choose between US (us-central-1) and EU (eu-west-1) at org creation. Data, backups, and derived rollups stay in the selected region. We do not cross-replicate telemetry data between regions.

For compliance-sensitive workloads, Eden offers dedicated cloud tenancy under enterprise contracts — contact sales@eden.dev. Eden is not distributed as a self-hosted control plane.

4. Retention and deletion

Trace events and judge scores retain for the period configured in your retention policy (default 90 days for trace events, 365 days for eval aggregates). On request we will hard-delete an entire organisation or a specific sub-tree of resources within 30 days — contact privacy@eden.dev.

Account deletion cascades: removing your user record deletes all sessions, personal tokens, and SSO bindings; the org's data is preserved unless the org owner separately requests deletion.

5. When we share data

We do not sell, rent, or share customer telemetry with third parties — including for training. Subprocessors (Postgres hosting, ClickHouse hosting, error reporting, email delivery) are listed in our DPA, which is auto-attached to enterprise contracts.

We may disclose data when required by law (subpoena, court order). In the 24 months ending June 2026 we have received zero such requests and disclosed zero customer records.

6. Your rights (GDPR · CCPA)

You can export, correct, or delete your data at any time from the settings page, or by emailing privacy@eden.dev. GDPR data-subject requests are acknowledged within 72 hours and fulfilled within 30 days. CCPA “Do Not Sell” is honoured by default (we never sell).
