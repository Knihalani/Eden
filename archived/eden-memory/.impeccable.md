## Design Context

### Users
Agent builders — solo or on a team. They sign up, wrap the agent, then live in a product dashboard and open a canvas to edit/Approve. Copy talks like a product. Clarity first. Landing exists to convert to Sign up without lying or dumping internals. Tagline: Making your agent do less work. Compiling tools is the first product, not the identity of the brand.

### Brand Personality
Night desk. Quiet, exact. Crimson for Sign up and Approve only. Live is a verb, not a color. Same hexagonal **e**. Not gold. Not mint. Not lavender. Not coral. Not ice. Not ochre.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark instrument (Linear/Vercel night, not a CRT). Graphite neutrals, hue ~260, chroma ~0.01. Landing is a brand surface: cinematic scroll, still honest. Dashboard breathes. Canvas is a darker well. Hairline chrome, luminance stacking, no drop-shadow stacks, no KPI card grids. Source Serif 4 for the promise, Bricolage Grotesque for UI. Crimson (cool red, hue ~18, high chroma) for ship only. No second chromatic. Home uses no ship color.

Anti-references: Signal Black + gold portal, IBM Plex wireframes, Fraunces highlighter-E, cream paper, KPI card grids, neon cyan-on-dark, Linear lavender, mint/garden/hue 155, coral, ice, forced product tours, empty canvas as home, fake metrics/logos, bounce/particle/gradient-text landings.

### Design Principles
1. Landing → sign up → short skippable tour → setup prompt → dashboard. Canvas is opened from a workflow or a request.
2. Same `logo.webp`. Never redraw the mark.
3. Approve on the canvas after editing. One chromatic CTA (crimson).
4. Flattened chrome: rail + well, not nested cards.
5. A runtime key never turns a tool On.
6. Setup prompt is placeholders unless they choose “include my keys.”
7. Workspace/org from the first session.
8. Promise-only marketing. No fake proof. No oversharing.
