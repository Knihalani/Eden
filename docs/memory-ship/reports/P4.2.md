## What changed
- **One ranker for every reader: D17's BM25F** (`src/memory-bm25f.ts`, `relevant` in `src/index.ts`).
  - The Submit's situation cue, SubagentStart, PreCompact and the wrap all rank the same way.
  - **Tokenizer:** R1's (`benchmarks/memory-cues/lex.ts`, the one D17's BM25F was measured with). A term is:
    - 2 characters or more;
    - not a stop word of R1's or of Eden's;
    - not a number of 1–2 digits;
    - plural-stemmed.
  - A row's text gives each compound identifier whole and split, and its dotted segments whole (`apps/web/middleware.ts`, `middleware.ts`, `apps`, `web`, …). A prompt keeps a dotted name whole (P4.1's rule). An apostrophe splits a word, as Eden's needles did ("Kai's" is "kai").
  - **Fields** (D17): value ×3 and subject ×2 from a row's stored D7 keys, aliases ×1.5 from the alias words its tags hold but its text does not, body ×1 from its text.
  - **Score:** R1's `idf·tf/(k1 + tf)`, k1 = 1.2, b = 0.75, field lengths normalised against their means (floored at 1). It is on R1's scale, so P4.3's τ can start from R1's points.
  - **Kind prior:** D17's bounded prior (a thousandth of the legacy one) everywhere. The legacy +25/+40/+50 prior is gone from SubagentStart, PreCompact and cue-less callers.
  - **Long rows:** a row the user typed ranks at any length, normalised for its length. A tool result or agent text of more than 12 distinct terms is still not ranked (P0.6's cost fix, now for untyped rows only).
  - Admission is unchanged until P4.3: a word shared with the prompt, or a continuation's STRONG identifier.
- **Bounded candidates** (A4 §5.1 step 4c; `candidateRows` in `src/memory-postings.ts`). |C| ≤ 200, built in this order:
  - a term in at most 64 visible rows gives them all, rarest first;
  - the 4 rarest fatter terms give the rows two of them share, newest first (scan cap 8,192 entries);
  - each fat term gives its newest 64 (walk cap 256).
- **The postings snapshot** (`postings.snap` beside the store; `src/memory-postings.ts`).
  - **Format:** one atomic file, written 0600. A header line holds row counts and field lengths by scope and a byte range per part; then the row ids with a digest each; then term-hashed buckets of per-scope ordinals.
  - **Reading:** a Submit reads only its cue's buckets, through one file descriptor, and only the lists of the scopes it may see (`visibleFrom(…).scopes`).
  - **The delta:** rows the snapshot does not hold by id are indexed in process. That index is kept in a long-lived process until its rows change.
  - **When it is written:**
    - the idle sleeper and `eden memory remember`: when 1 row waits;
    - the light sleep: when 256 rows wait;
    - either one: when a bucket cannot be read.

    A waiting row is one the snapshot does not hold, one it holds whose text, tags or keys changed, or one it holds that the store dropped or took back.
  - A snapshot that cannot be read is set aside; the rows are indexed in process.
  - Every purge deletes the snapshots.
  - The legacy `sleep-postings.jsonl`, which nothing read, is removed (ROADMAP hygiene).
- **D33's hot path** (`trimToStoreCap`), your choice of 2026-10-16:
  - a store keeps its newest 20,000 rows (`EDEN_MEMORY_STORE_CAP`; it was 2,000), pins and constraints always;
  - older rows are appended to `diary.jsonl`, which no hook reads;
  - the seen-text sets are built lazily;
  - the light sleep reads the newest 2,000 live rows, the store's whole size before; its identity pass took 2–3 s at every Stop at 20k rows.
- **A4 §5.1 step 0, the budget abort** (`packages/cli/src/memory-hook.ts`, `memory-hook-io.ts`).
  - A human Submit past 150 ms from its handler's start (`EDEN_SUBMIT_BUDGET_MS`; 0 or `off`: none) is aborted, at either of two points:
    - before retrieval: the prompt is still encoded;
    - before the ledger write: nothing is recorded as delivered.
  - It prints nothing and logs `aborted: true`, and the breaker counts it.
  - An in-process caller has no budget.
  - The clock starts after Bun's start and the module load, your choice of 2026-10-16. Counted from the process's start, as first built and as Target 3 is worded, it aborted 7/500 Submits on a 2,000-row store.
  - The A5 harness runs hooks with a 1 s budget (`fixture:` 966f416ad).

## Decisions made
- **Tokenizer:** R1's (`lex.ts`), the one D17's BM25F was measured with. Offline over the 15 CI needs, it met 15/15 at 2,000 and 20,000 rows against Eden's needles' 13/15. An apostrophe splits a word (D17 is silent; Eden's needles did).
- **No 0.7·top cut** until P4.3's τ (A4 §5.1 step 6 pairs them). On world 4 it removed 19 false bullets and no need, but it broke D26's residual interplay and several tests.
- **D17's fields** come from the stored D7 keys (value ×3, subject ×2), alias ×1.5 from the alias words a row's tags hold and its text lacks, body ×1.
- **The 12-term cut** stays for untyped rows (tool results, agent text), P0.6's cost fix. Typed rows rank at any length (Target 4).
- **The harness budget is 1 s** (`fixture:` 966f416ad): many hooks at once make load, not Eden, abort Submits.
- **The light sleep** reads the newest 2,000 live rows; at 20k it took 2–3 s per Stop.
- **The budget clock starts at the handler** (A4 §5.1 step 0, where "budget timer" sits), your choice. A2 §2.8 had suggested "e.g. 150 ms wall from process start". It starts after the payload is read from stdin.
- **`scale-1e6` `on` green at CI scale:** its CI-scale run is `scale-20k`'s 2,000 rows; its 10⁶ rows are P6.1's.
