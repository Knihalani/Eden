## What changed
- **A7 §4's evidence gate on the lexical path** (A4 §5.1 step 6), for every reader (Submit, SubagentStart, PreCompact, the wrap), behind a new flag `gate.tau` (`EDEN_FLAG_GATE_TAU`), **shadow by default**. You chose shadow on 2026-10-17, after code review round 1 showed the strict gate's recall cost.
  - **`on`** admits a row on STRONG evidence: an identifier-shaped token it shares with the prompt, or on a continuation with the agent's last message only. Otherwise it admits on the MEDIUM path, which needs three things:
    - MEDIUM evidence: a prompt word that at most 50 visible rows hold, or any word of a context part. WEAK words never admit.
    - The unweighted sum of the cue parts' BM25F reaching τ_repo.
    - A ranking score of at least 0.7 × the MEDIUM path's top score (D17's cut). A STRONG row sets no top, nor does a stored copy of the prompt.
    - An episode needs MEDIUM from two cue parts as well. An episode is a row the user did not type, or, with the gate on, a typed request stored as an episode.
    - The gate reads the prompt identifier-split (A7 §3): "Node.js" gives node.js, node and js.
    - **Names in prose are words to the gate** (the iteration, amendment 6). A name that starts with a capital and has an inner one ("TypeScript", "GitHub", "BullMQ"), and a product named like a script ("Next.js"), are words, MEDIUM at most. A camelCase symbol (`noUncheckedIndexedAccess`), a path, a file, an error code, a flag, a version and an id stay STRONG.
    - **The MEDIUM path needs two words** (the iteration). One rare word never admits a row, however high it scores on a short row. A word is the prompt token a term came from, split at whitespace and hyphens, so "TypeScript"'s parts are one word and "code-first" is two.
  - **`shadow`** admits exactly as `main` does. The Submit's wide event records what the gate saw: `gate` {τ, rows with evidence, top sum, STRONG and MEDIUM admits, `drop` = rows `main` admits that `on` would not}.
  - **`off`** computes nothing.
- **τ_repo** (`src/memory-tau.ts`) is R1's strict point (4.1 on R1's fixture, whose mean idf is 4.55) rescaled by the store's mean idf. The postings snapshot header now carries the mean idf; a snapshot written before P4.3 lacks it and is rebuilt at the next sleep. `EDEN_MEMORY_TAU` overrides τ.
- **τ's refit at full sleep** (A7 §4):
