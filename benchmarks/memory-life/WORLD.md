## Areas memories may cover

- Preferences: food, drink, travel, shopping, communication style, how the agent should answer.
- People and relationships: who is who, birthdays, allergies, diets, likes and dislikes, sensitivities.
- Plans and commitments: dated events, promises made to people, deadlines (some already past by "now").
- Routines: weekly schedules, school pickups, workouts, lessons.
- Past decisions and their reasons.
- Corrections: a later fact replaces an older one (the older one stays as a superseded row).
- Standing instructions to the agent ("always…", "never…", "when I ask for X, do Y").
- Work: projects, colleagues, team norms, metrics, launch dates.
- Learning: Spanish progress, mistakes Riya tends to make, how she likes to be taught.
- Health and wellbeing: conditions, medications, stress triggers (handled with care).
- Home and money: renovation, budget, accounts, subscriptions, car (2019 Mazda CX-5).
- Noise the store also holds, as a real passive memory would: diary rows of one-off past requests ("asked for a summary of an article about…"), and rows of tool output saved as memory (weather lookups, search results, order confirmations). Most of these are never needed again.
